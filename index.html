Privacy Policy for Nafis

Last updated: 11 July 2026

Nafis ("the app") is a private price tracker for precious metals, currencies, and crypto — showing local retail prices, a personal portfolio, zakat, alerts, and per-country news. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Our core principle

Nafis has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. We do not operate any servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. All of your settings, holdings, and portfolio data are stored locally on your device. The only information that ever leaves your device is the anonymous network requests for public market data and news described below.

Your portfolio and holdings

The assets you add (metals, coins, crypto, weights, quantities, making charges, and prices) are stored only on your device in a local database. They are never uploaded, synced, or shared. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it.

Location data

If you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to determine your country so it can show the right local prices (for example, the Egyptian صاغة gold price) and localized news. Your coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net) to convert them into a country and city name for display. These coordinates are used only to fulfil that request — they are not stored by Nafis, are not linked to your identity, and are never used for advertising. If you deny location permission, you can still choose your country and currency manually and use the app fully.

Market data and news requests

To show live prices and headlines, the app fetches public data from the services below. These requests contain no personal information about you — only the asset or topic being looked up:

  • Precious-metal spot prices — api.gold-api.com
  • Currency exchange rates — open.er-api.com
  • Cryptocurrency prices — api.coingecko.com
  • City search (manual location) — geocoding-api.open-meteo.com
  • News headlines — Google News RSS (news.google.com)

Notifications

Price alerts you set are evaluated and delivered entirely on your device. The app does not use remote push notifications and does not send your device token to any server.

Children's privacy

Nafis does not knowingly collect personal information from children and is not directed at children under 13.

Third-party services

The third-party services listed above process the requests described. Their handling of network requests is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • BigDataCloud — bigdatacloud.com
  • Gold-API — gold-api.com
  • ExchangeRate-API — exchangerate-api.com
  • CoinGecko — coingecko.com
  • Open-Meteo — open-meteo.com
  • Google News — policies.google.com/privacy

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
