Questions

Is Nafis free?

Nafis is free to use, with no ads. Nafis Pro is an optional in-app purchase that adds cloud sync, family vaults and server-side alerts.

Which gold price does Nafis show?

The local buy and sell price per gram for 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K. It blends international spot with the local market dollar, so it matches your jeweller rather than a far-off exchange.

Which countries does it cover?

Nafis is tuned for Egypt and the Gulf: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman.

Does Nafis work offline?

Yes. Prices are cached, so it opens instantly and keeps working without a connection.

Is Nafis available in Arabic?

Yes. Nafis is fully in Arabic and English, with a proper right-to-left layout.

Which phones can run Nafis?

Nafis is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. Home-screen widgets and launcher shortcuts are Android-only.
